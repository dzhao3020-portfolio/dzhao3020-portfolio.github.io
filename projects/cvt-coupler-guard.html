CVT coupler guard

Baja SAE rules require guarding around rotating drivetrain parts. I designed the guard for the high-speed coupler between the CVT and the gearbox, sized to contain a coupler or U-joint failure in a very tight space.

Team
Northwestern Baja SAE
Role
Designer
Dates
Jan 2025 to Apr 2025
Tools
SolidWorks sheet metal, top-down assembly design
Status
Integrated on the car

Designing in place

I modeled the guard directly inside the top-level vehicle assembly. Working top-down let me build a precise envelope around the coupler and shafts, and I ran interference detection throughout to hold a consistent clearance from everything that moves or vibrates. The guard also had to come off quickly for service.

Designed to be cut and bent

  • Material. I chose a steel gauge for impact resistance and containment over weight, since safety was the point of the part.
  • Bends. I specified bend radii and reliefs so the part wouldn’t crack while forming.
  • Flat pattern. The key output was the flat pattern, exported as a DXF straight to the waterjet or laser cutter.

Outcome

The finished guard fits closely around the coupler and integrates into a heavily constrained part of the car. It was my first full pass through sheet metal design, from modeling around existing parts to the flat pattern that drives fabrication.
