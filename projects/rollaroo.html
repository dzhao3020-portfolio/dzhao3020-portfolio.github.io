Design Thinking and Communication, Spring 2025

Rollaroo: a foldable ball return

A ball-return toy for a local kindergarten that had to work for two very different users: children ages 2 to 5, and the staff who set it up, take it down, and carry it upstairs every week.

Program
Design Thinking and Communication, Northwestern
Team
4 students
Role
Structure, folding, and assembly design
Dates
Mar 2025 to Jun 2025
Status
Design freeze

Constraints from the people who’d use it

From staff interviews and classroom observation, I helped define the engineering constraints:

  • No more than 20 lb.
  • Set up in under 3 minutes.
  • Tool-free, by one person.
  • No wood, metal, or similarly hard materials.

Safe for kids

I sketched and developed the core structure and pushed for lightweight PVC pipe to meet both the weight limit and the no-wood rule. A wide base keeps it from tipping, and every point a child could hit is soft.

Easy for staff

I designed the central folding hinge and telescoping legs to be intuitive and tool-free, so one person can collapse it, carry it up a flight of stairs, and store it compactly.

Outcome

We reached a design freeze ready for fabrication, plus a video showing staff how it works and why it’s built the way it is. The biggest takeaway was designing for the whole life of a product, from playtime to cleanup and storage.
